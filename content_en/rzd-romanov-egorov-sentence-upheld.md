The Moscow City Court has upheld the sentences of Alexey Romanov, former head of the communications network construction directorate of Russian Railways (RZD), and his first deputy Sergey Egorov. Moscow's Basmanny District Court had sentenced each of them to seven years in a general-regime penal colony for abuse of office under Part 3 of Article 285 of the Criminal Code. The defense appeals were rejected, the FSB's public relations center and media reported on November 14.

The story began in 2016. Under a program to modernize the "Kuzbass — Far Eastern transport hub" route, RZD signed contracts with OOO PSK Remput to lay track superstructure on the Mezhdurechensk–Taishet section of the Krasnoyarsk Railway. The contracts were worth more than 1.6 billion rubles in total.

The contractor received more than 751 million rubles in advances. It did not complete the work and did not return the unearned part of the advances. According to investigators, Romanov and Egorov approved the advances in breach of established procedures.

The case was opened on December 30, 2020, and investigated by the Main Investigative Directorate of the Investigative Committee. Both men were detained on March 16, 2023: Romanov was remanded in custody and Egorov was placed under house arrest.

The victim, the Federal Agency for Railway Transport, has filed a civil claim against the convicted men for more than 383 million rubles. Separately, in anti-corruption proceedings brought by prosecutors, property worth more than 350 million rubles was seized from Romanov and his relatives and turned over to the state.
