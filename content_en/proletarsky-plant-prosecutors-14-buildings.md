The Arbitration Court of St. Petersburg and the Leningrad Region has frozen 14 buildings and land plots on Dudko Street in the city's Nevsky district at the request of prosecutors, who want them returned to PAO Proletarsky Zavod, Fontanka reported. The Telegram channel Agenda was first to report the claim.

The court imposed the arrest on November 10 as an interim measure. The current owners, companies and sole proprietors, may not sell or damage the property. The first hearing on the merits is scheduled for January 20.

The claim was filed by a deputy St. Petersburg prosecutor. Prosecutors ask the court to apply the consequences of invalidity to void sale contracts signed in 2007–2011 and to restore the plant's ownership of the 14 buildings. According to Fontanka, the claim does not explain why prosecutors consider the deals void. The plant itself, United Shipbuilding Corporation (USC), ZAO TsNIISM, the federal property agency Rosimushchestvo and OOO YurFinConsultant are third parties. USC declined to comment.

By Fontanka's count, the dispute covers about 23,900 square meters of buildings and premises and 19,700 square meters of land with a combined cadastral value of 466.6 million rubles. The largest owner is OOO Izolyatsionnye Polimernye Materialy, owned by Anatoly and Valentina Yurush: three buildings, premises and three plots with a cadastral value of about 194 million rubles. Three more buildings belong to OOO Polaris-Neva, which in 2020 leased property to Proletarsky Zavod itself. Buildings are also held by OOO Parusa, OOO Kompas, OOO Simurg and two sole proprietors.

This is not the only prosecutors' claim involving the plant. In the summer, they asked a court to recognize the company's ownership of about 1% of its own shares, which workers received in the 1991 privatization. The next hearing in that case is on November 30.

Prosecutors in St. Petersburg have recently challenged the privatization of several properties, from the Palace of Labor to the Kirov House of Culture. And on November 12, an appeal court rejected the Prosecutor General's bid to seize 45% of [Petersburg Oil Terminal](https://kursmedia.com/en/articles/petersburg-oil-terminal-appeal.html).
