The Moscow Arbitration Court has placed Timur Akhmerov, chief executive of Bars Group, into debt restructuring. The ruling was issued by Judge Alexander Svirin, the outlet Kazanfirst reported. The bankruptcy petition was filed by the National Informatization Center, a Rostec unit. It is claiming 1.45 billion rubles: 866.7 million in principal and 578.2 million in interest. Vladimir Chechenko has been appointed financial manager.

Bars Group has operated since 1992 and is considered one of Russia's largest developers of software for the state. Its main focus is digital healthcare: medical information systems, components of the federal VIMIS platform and compulsory health insurance systems. Akhmerov has led the company since 1999.

The debt stems from a deal made a decade ago. In 2016, Akhmerov sold the National Informatization Center 25% plus one share of Bars Group for 350 million rubles. Under a shareholders' agreement, he undertook to buy the stake back if the company fell short of agreed financial targets by more than 25%. In 2019, revenue missed the plan by 25.6%, and the center demanded the buyback.

Restructuring is the first stage of personal bankruptcy. The debtor has time to propose a repayment plan to creditors. If the plan is not approved or not carried out, the court may move to the sale of assets. Published reports do not include the positions of Akhmerov or Bars Group.
