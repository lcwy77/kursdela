VK has filed a claim against the Council of the EU in the General Court of the European Union. The case, VK v. Council, was registered on October 7, 2026, under number T-664/26. The proceedings are at an early stage, and the sanctions remain in force.

**VK's position.** The company's press office: "VK considers the decision unlawful and unfounded and demands that the sanctions imposed earlier be lifted."

**The EU's position.** VK was added to the sanctions list on July 13, together with the developer of the Max messenger. Brussels said the company allegedly contributes to human rights violations because its technologies are used to monitor users. That is the EU's wording.

The sanctions mean an asset freeze in the EU and a ban on European companies providing VK with funds and economic resources. In July, the holding said this would not affect the operation of its services.

Something else did. In late June, VK's products disappeared from the App Store, and on July 16 VK and Max became unavailable on Google Play. Apps already installed still work, but a new smartphone user has nowhere to download them.

The claim looks more like a way to put the company's position on record than to get its apps back into the stores: Apple and Google are not subject to the EU court, and even a win in Luxembourg would not automatically reverse their decisions.

**Update, November 16, 2026. A second front: against Apple.** In parallel, VK is suing Apple in the Moscow Arbitration Court. It wants the VKontakte, Odnoklassniki and VK Music apps restored to the App Store, push notifications reinstated and a penalty for non-compliance. On October 20, Apple went to the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California, arguing that its developer license agreement requires such disputes to be heard there under California law. On October 21, the California court issued a temporary restraining order without hearing VK.

On October 30, the Moscow Arbitration Court, at VK's request, responded in kind: it barred Apple Inc., Apple Distribution International and Apple Rus LLC from continuing the proceedings in the United States and ordered them to take steps within seven days to end them. On November 6, Apple asked the Moscow court to lift the ban. A preliminary hearing on VK's claim is scheduled for December 8. No timetable has been announced for the case against the Council of the EU in Luxembourg.
