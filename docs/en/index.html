    Shelf price vs. register price: FTC and 20 states sue Dollar General over overcharges

    The Federal Trade Commission and 20 states have sued Dollar General, alleging that the 21,000-store discount chain charged shoppers more at the register than its shelf prices for years. The company has paid six states $6 million over similar claims since 2019.

    A Russian court has ordered Rio Tinto to pay Rusal $1.278 billion over its exclusion from an Australian alumina refinery and has levied execution on Rio's 66% stake in the operator of Mongolia's Oyu Tolgoi copper mine. The parties' positions and what comes next.

    Rusnano v. Chubais: bailiffs pursue nearly 20 billion rubles over a failed school tablet and MRAM chips

    Russia's Federal Bailiff Service has opened 14 enforcement proceedings against former Rusnano chief Anatoly Chubais worth nearly 20 billion rubles (about $240 million). They stem from Moscow Arbitration Court rulings on losses from the Plastic Logic and Crocus projects.
